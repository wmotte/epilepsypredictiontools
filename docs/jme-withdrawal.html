Seizure recurrence risk after drug withdrawal in JME
ⓘ

A third of people with juvenile myoclonic epilepsy (JME) are drug-resistant, and three-quarters have a seizure relapse when attempting to withdraw antiseizure medication after achieving seizure-freedom. We identified predictors of seizure recurrence to allow for individualised prediction of treatment outcomes in people with JME.
