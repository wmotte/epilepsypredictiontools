About this calculator

This lookup table is based on clinical nomograms from the TimeToStop study group [1]. The nomogram estimates the risk of seizure recurrence and long-term seizure outcome for children with epilepsy who underwent epilepsy surgery, in case of withdrawal of antiepileptic drugs with the purpose of discontinuing treatment. The prediction model is based on data from 766 children who underwent epilepsy surgery in 15 centres in 8 European countries.

Reference:

Lamberink HJ, Boshuisen K, Otte WM, Geleijns K, Braun KPJ, on behalf of the TimeToStop Study Group. Individualized prediction of seizure relapse and outcomes following antiepileptic drug withdrawal after pediatric epilepsy surgery. Epilepsia 2018. DOI: 10.1111/epi.14020
