Developmental outcome following pediatric surgery

What is the probability of obtaining a developmental quotient of 50 or higher following pediatric epilepsy surgery?

The following definitions are used:

MCD: all subtypes of focal cortical dysplasia except those in the context of tuberous sclerosis complex
vascular: perinatal infarction and porencephaly, arteriovenous malformations and cavernoma
encephalitis: infection-related and autoimmune, such as Rasmussen encephalitis

About this calculator

This lookup table is based on clinical nomograms from a collaborative study between Universitätsklinik OWL and the UMC Utrecht to identify predictors of postoperative developmental quotients (DQ) and develop and validate clinically applicable prediction models two years after pediatric epilepsy surgery. It provides the performance post-surgery.

Reference:

Cloppenborg T, van Schooneveld MMJ, Hagemann A, Hopf JL, Kalbhenn T, Otte WM, Polster T, Bien CG, Braun KPJ. Development and validation of prediction models for developmental and intellectual outcome following pediatric epilepsy surgery. Neurology 2022; 98:e225–e235. DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000013065
